# Privacy Policy for Garden Pro

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

Rafael (the "Developer", "we", "us", or "our") built the Garden Pro app (the "App") as a Freemium app. This SERVICE is provided by Rafael and is intended for use "as is".

This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

## Data Collection and Use
Garden Pro is designed to respect your privacy. The core functionality of the App operates **completely offline**, meaning all your plant records, tasks, zones, and gardening data are stored securely and locally on your own device. We do not require you to create an account, and **we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal gardening data to our servers.**

However, the App uses third-party services for monetization (displaying ads and processing subscriptions). These services may collect certain information automatically.

**Third-Party Service Providers used by the App:**
*   [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)
*   [Google Play Billing](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice)

These third-party providers may automatically collect certain device information, such as:
*   Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
*   Device ID and advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Ad ID)
*   Operating system version and device model
*   Crash logs and anonymous usage statistics

## Advertising
We use **Google AdMob** to display ads in the free version of the App. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests. You can manage or opt-out of personalized advertising through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization).

## In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
To unlock unlimited features and remove ads, the App offers subscriptions via **Google Play Billing**. All payment transactions are handled entirely and securely by Google. We do not process your payments directly, and we do not have access to your credit card or financial information.

## Security
Since all your app data is stored locally on your device, the security of this data relies on the security of your device itself. We recommend securing your phone with a passcode or biometric lock.

## Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
